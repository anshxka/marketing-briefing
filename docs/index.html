DigidayMars

Mars Prepares for Agentic AI Commerce as Digital Assistants Eye the Grocery Run

Mars is actively rethinking its commerce and retail strategies as autonomous AI shopping assistants prepare to manage routine grocery shopping. Drawing from a recent retail study conducted alongside Kantar and Synthesis, Neil Reynolds, global chief customer officer at Mars, expects agentic tech to oversee weekly pantry replenishment, shifting the role of physical stores toward discovery and entertainment.

To safeguard impulse purchases for brands such as M&Ms, Dove, and Pop-Tarts, the snack giant is pursuing a two-tier roadmap. The company is focusing on ensuring its products are discoverable and readily convert within large language models (LLMs), while simultaneously partnering with physical retailers to transform in-store environments into experiential and immersive destinations.

Mars is currently working with emerging technology partners to gather baseline data on how its categories surface inside LLMs and where that training data originates. Internally, the company is assembling cross-functional 'One Demand performance squads' designed to unify brand marketing and commerce teams in navigating agentic consumer journeys.

Why it mattersAs autonomous AI agents begin making shopping decisions, brands must master algorithmic discoverability just as they once mastered physical endcap placement. Marketers need to integrate brand equity with technical LLM optimization to stay on automated shopping lists.

Creators Question TwitchCon's ROI Amid Safety Concerns and Shifting Attendance

TwitchCon is facing growing scrutiny from creators ahead of its gathering in San Diego, as high costs, safety worries, and an uncertain return on investment challenge its historical appeal. The convention has long served as a flagship destination for Amazon-owned Twitch—which accumulated 900 million hours of watch time last year—bringing together streamers, fans, and major sponsors including State Farm, e.l.f. Cosmetics, Honda, and Southwest Airlines.

However, top-performing Twitch Partners have reportedly seen lower ticket participation numbers, accompanied by online claims that the platform resorted to ticket giveaways via its Ambassador program. While Twitch noted that live event dynamics have shifted following post-COVID peak demand, creators and agencies question the event's current purpose. Justin Miclat, chief growth officer at Click Media, noted that it is difficult to determine how to add value to TwitchCon's current programming after spending $50,000 on an on-site showcase event last year.

The event has also encountered severe scrutiny around physical safety and conduct. Following an assault on top streamer Emily-Beth 'Emiru' Schunk during a meet-and-greet at TwitchCon 2025, attendees have raised concerns regarding security protocols and disruptive behavior from IRL streamers around host venues, making creators reconsider whether the commercial upside justifies the expense and risks.

Why it mattersBrand marketers relying on creator-led events must reassess whether legacy conventions still offer the engaged talent pool and safe environment required for high-budget activations. When top creators pull back from flagship gatherings due to weak ROI and security issues, sponsors risk spending premium dollars on diminished audience reach.
