Inc42Zetwerk

Zetwerk Sets ₹143–₹151 Price Band for ₹5,012 Crore IPO

B2B manufacturing unicorn Zetwerk has fixed its price band between ₹143 and ₹151 per share for its upcoming ₹5,012.3 crore initial public offering. At the upper end of the band, the issue targets a post-issue valuation of approximately ₹25,583 crore (around $2.7 billion). The public subscription opens on October 15 and closes on October 19, following anchor investor bidding scheduled for October 14.

The offering comprises a fresh issue of ₹2,830 crore alongside an offer for sale (OFS) of up to 14.45 crore shares worth ₹2,182.3 crore. Both components have been expanded since the initial filings, with the fresh issue rising from ₹2,600 crore and the OFS expanding by 49.2%. Co-founders Amrit Pratik Acharya and Srinath Ramakkrushnan will each offload up to 1.42 crore shares, with early investors such as Peak XV Partners, Accel India, Lightspeed Venture Partners, and Kae Capital also participating.

A significant portion of the primary capital will go toward balance sheet optimization. Zetwerk intends to deploy ₹2,160 crore—roughly 76% of the fresh issue—to repay or prepay existing borrowings across the company and selected subsidiaries, with the remaining capital earmarked for acquisitions and general corporate purposes.

Why it mattersZetwerk’s IPO highlights how large B2B startups are prioritizing debt reduction and balance sheet strength to build credibility with public investors. For marketers, communicating financial stability and operational discipline is becoming as critical as marketing growth metrics.

Inc42Ola Electric

Bhavish Aggarwal Pledges 4.32% Ola Electric Stake to Back Rights Issue

Ola Electric founder Bhavish Aggarwal has pledged 20 crore shares, representing a 4.32% stake in the EV company, against non-convertible debentures issued by his AI entity, Krutrim Data Centre. Held by CTL Trusteeship, this marks a larger pledge than his previous encumbrance of 17.3 crore shares, which was cleared in December 2025 after selling a 3.93% stake to repay a ₹260 crore loan.

Ola Electric stated that the fresh encumbrance is solely intended to fund Aggarwal’s participation in the EV firm's upcoming ₹1,000 crore rights issue without selling equity. However, governance advisors note that repeated pledge-and-release cycles can signal promoter stress, coming just four months after the automaker raised ₹780 crore via a qualified institutional placement in June 2026.

The fundraising push occurs amidst competitive headwinds in the electric two-wheeler market. While Ola Electric's market share recovered from 5.1% in Q4 FY26 to 8.4% in Q1 FY27, it remains below its 15% to 20% target. In contrast, rival Ather delivered 83,173 scooters during the same quarter—more than double Ola’s 39,192 units—while posting lower financial losses as Ola works to scale its dealership network and gigafactory.

Why it mattersRepeated promoter share pledging and frequent capital calls risk eroding stakeholder confidence and brand perception in high-visibility tech sectors. Brand custodians must ensure corporate governance narratives match operational realities to safeguard long-term brand equity.
