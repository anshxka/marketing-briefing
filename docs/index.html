  1. India's digital and transaction rails are operating at an unprecedented scale—highlighted by UPI's record 25 billion monthly transactions and rapid B2B quick-commerce growth—creating a frictionless foundation for brands to capture high-intent consumer spend across retail touchpoints.

ET BrandEquityInstagram

Instagram Tests 90-Minute Videos to Strengthen Platform Retention

Instagram is reportedly experimenting with video uploads as long as 90 minutes, a move that could fundamentally shift how the platform manages user engagement. While industry discussions frequently frame the update as a direct challenge to YouTube, the strategy echoes traditional television concepts such as audience flow, where a successful programme is designed to retain viewers and prevent them from reaching for the exit button.

Currently, discovery often happens on Instagram through formats like Reels, but audiences frequently migrate elsewhere—such as heading to YouTube to watch a full-length music performance or video. By facilitating full-length video, Instagram is attempting to host the entire journey within its own ecosystem, turning momentary discovery into sustained viewing time.

The strategic shift mirrors a classic business challenge: balancing acquisition with retention. Much like how companies such as Apple and Amazon focus on delivering ongoing value after initial conversion, social platforms and digital businesses are recognizing that top-of-funnel discovery must be backed by reasons for audiences to stay.

Why it mattersMarketers must focus on audience retention and lifetime value rather than treating top-of-funnel discovery as the final objective. If your short-form content drives discovery, ensure you have the assets in place to capture deeper engagement before the consumer exits.
