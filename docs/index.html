Inc42BigBasket

FSSAI Issues Show-Cause Notices to BigBasket and Flipkart Over Misleading Cooking Oil Claims

The Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) has issued show-cause notices to ecommerce and quick commerce platforms BigBasket and Flipkart, alongside manufacturer Gemini Edibles & Fats India. The action targets alleged misleading promotions for "Freedom Cooking Rice Bran Oil," which claimed to reduce bad cholesterol, maintain or increase good cholesterol, and contain over 10,000 PPM Natural Oryzanol. Under the Food Safety and Standards (Advertising and Claims) Regulations, 2018, associating edible vegetable oils with heart health is strictly prohibited. The regulator also noted that Gemini was selling food via its proprietary website without holding a valid Central Licence covering ecommerce activity.

This development comes as the FSSAI prepares dedicated regulatory frameworks for ecommerce, quick commerce, and cloud kitchens. The proposed norms intend to make platforms directly accountable for misleading manufacturer claims while mandating the online display of manufacturing dates, expiry dates, and batch numbers. Additional measures under review include stricter checks on dark store storage conditions and mandatory disclosures of cloud kitchen photographs and physical addresses.

The notices follow an escalating crackdown on digital grocery channels. FSSAI recently penalized Amazon, BigBasket, Flipkart, Swiggy Instamart, and Zepto over misbranding and the display of prohibited articles, alongside earlier notices to Instamart regarding spoiled products. Concurrently, state enforcement has intensified, with Maharashtra's FDA suspending licences for 14 dark stores and Karnataka officials sealing a Zepto warehouse in Bengaluru over regulatory breaches.

Why it mattersEcommerce and quick commerce marketplaces can no longer rely on intermediary safe harbors to shield themselves from manufacturer-created product copy and health claims. Performance and brand marketers must audit product descriptions and packaging claims across all retail channels to ensure strict compliance with FSSAI regulations.

ET BrandEquityHyundai Motor India

Hyundai Motor India Reframes Disability Narrative with ‘Samarth’ Accessibility Initiative

Hyundai Motor India is driving purpose-led marketing through its flagship accessibility initiative, ‘Samarth by Hyundai’. Conceptualized under the automaker's global framework "Progress for Humanity," the initiative addresses the systemic challenges faced by India’s community of over 26.8 million persons with disabilities (PwDs). Rather than leaning into conventional CSR narratives centered on charity, pity, or "inspiration porn," the automaker set out to address accessibility as an issue of structural design.

According to Virat Khullar, head of marketing at Hyundai Motor India, the strategic pivot relied on shifting the conversation from empathy to structural access. By viewing people with disabilities as individuals seeking accessible systems to live independently with dignity—rather than passive beneficiaries—Hyundai converted standard CSR awareness into a multi-phase, systemic intervention that treats accessibility as a shared societal responsibility.

The campaign was constructed around two complementary pillars, with the initial phase focusing on shifting public perception and building nationwide awareness. Hyundai partnered with Shah Rukh Khan as the initiative's primary ambassador, using documentaries, telethons, community storytelling, and strategic media partnerships to build visibility. Ground-level efforts also included school engagement programs that have sensitized more than 125,000 students to date.

Why it mattersPurpose marketing falls flat when it relies on superficial sympathy rather than addressing structural, real-world problems. Hyundai demonstrates that brands can create authentic social equity by uniting mass-reach ambassadors with measurable, systemic community interventions.
