The Verge AIPerplexity

Perplexity Debuts 'Teammates' AI Agents for 24/7 Enterprise Workflows

Perplexity is stepping into direct competition with OpenAI and Anthropic by introducing always-on, workplace-focused AI agents named "Teammates." The agents are designed to adopt multiple roles across an organization, tapping into existing projects, skills, and memory stored within the user's Perplexity tools to operate continuously 24/7.

The feature builds on Perplexity Computer, an AI system that deploys agents to complete tasks on user devices. Teammates expands this capability to handle collaborative, large-scale, and ongoing work, enabling users to assign distinct jobs to multiple agents simultaneously. Teams can track workflows through individual threads inside the Perplexity app or communicate directly with the agents in Slack.

The new capability comes with a premium price point, restricted to users on Perplexity’s $200 per month Max tier. Highlighting the company's long-term enterprise ambitions, CEO Aravind Srinivas posted on X that the company envisions organizations eventually having hundreds of AI teammates working on behalf of each individual employee.

Why it mattersAlways-on autonomous agents have the potential to take over routine research, reporting, and operational task-switching for marketing departments. However, the steep $200 monthly cost per seat means marketing leaders must prove tangible operational ROI before expanding access across teams.
