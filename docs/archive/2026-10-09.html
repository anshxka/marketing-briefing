ET BrandEquityCleverTap

CleverTap Introduces CleverAI to Power Real-Time 1:1 Marketing Decisioning

CleverTap has unveiled the next generation of CleverAI, an engine combining AI decisioning and agentic automation to deliver real-time 1:1 personalization. The system integrates historical customer context with live behavior to autonomously decide which experience to trigger, via which channel, and at what exact moment, adhering to marketer-defined goals and guardrails. Pilot testing across enterprise clients demonstrated measurable improvements in transaction rates.

At the core of the platform are the "Tesseract Twins," consisting of TesseractDB and the Tesseract Decisioning Engine. TesseractDB retains historical and active context, while the decisioning engine analyzes the data to choose specific customer experiences. Each user outcome continuously feeds back into the system to refine future actions, supported by agentic tools that automatically generate copy, creative variations, campaigns, and customer journeys.

To meet enterprise standards, CleverAI includes data clean-room technology, governance protocols, frequency management, user-level inspection, and explainability controls. CleverTap leadership indicated that the new decisioning framework is positioned as a primary business driver rather than an incremental update, addressing the challenge of orchestrating interactions autonomously before live moments pass.

Why it mattersAutonomous decisioning allows retention and growth marketers to move beyond static triggers toward truly contextual, real-time engagement. For brands, embedding AI agents into creative and journey orchestration cuts campaign deployment time while lifting conversion rates.

World Bank reports that only 23% of surveyed Indian formal firms use AI despite surging investments

A recent World Bank report reveals a significant paradox in India's technology sector: while overall investments in artificial intelligence have surged dramatically from $1.2 billion in 2024 to $4.1 billion in 2025, actual adoption remains surprisingly low. Only 23 percent of surveyed Indian formal firms currently utilize AI in their operations.

This adoption rate sits at roughly one-third of the level recorded in the United States, highlighting a stark gap between capital deployment and ground-level enterprise integration within the country's formal business ecosystem.

As investments continue to pour into the market, industry stakeholders will be watching to see how enterprises bridge this implementation gap and translate funding growth into widespread operational utility.

Why it mattersMarketers must recognize that massive macro investments in technology do not automatically translate to immediate behavioral shifts in B2B markets. Brands targeting formal enterprises need to focus on education and reducing friction to drive actual product adoption rather than relying solely on category hype.
