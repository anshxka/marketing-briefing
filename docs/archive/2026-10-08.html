ET BrandEquityClevertap

Clevertap launches CleverAI to drive real-time personalization and decisioning

Clevertap has unveiled its latest offering, CleverAI, designed to combine AI decisioning and agentic automation for live 1:1 personalization. The system integrates historical customer context with live behavior to dynamically determine the right experience, channel, and moment for each user against specific marketer goals. Co-founder and CMO Anand Jain noted that executing a pre-decided action instantly does not constitute real-time decisioning; the harder problem is autonomously deciding afresh, customer by customer.

At the core of the platform are the 'Tesseract Twins': TesseractDB, which maintains historical and live customer data, and the Tesseract Decisioning Engine, which selects individual experiences and creates a continuous learning loop. Additionally, agentic capabilities support the creation of copy, creative variations, and customer journeys. The enterprise-grade system also features explainability, frequency controls, user-level inspection, and clean-room capabilities.

Tested through pilot programs with multiple clients, CleverAI yielded noteworthy improvements in transaction rates. Co-founder and CEO Sunil Thomas stated that the company expects the new offering to become a significant driver of its future growth.

Why it mattersMarketers can leverage real-time AI decisioning to autonomously orchestrate customer journeys moment by moment rather than relying on pre-scheduled workflows. This capability helps brands improve engagement and transaction rates by delivering hyper-personalized experiences at scale.

MediaNamaMeesho

CCPA Fines Meesho Rs 10 Lakh for Selling Unregistered Herbicide and Rejects Intermediary Defence

The Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA) has imposed a penalty of Rs 10 lakh on e-commerce platform Meesho. The fine was levied due to the platform allowing the sale of an unregistered herbicide. According to the authority, Meesho failed to properly verify sellers' licences and did not prevent misleading listings on its marketplace.

Along with the financial penalty, the CCPA has ordered a comprehensive, platform-wide audit of Meesho to address these compliance failures. The authority explicitly rejected the company's intermediary defence, signaling stricter accountability for e-commerce platforms regarding the third-party products listed and sold through their digital infrastructure.

Why it mattersE-commerce marketplaces can no longer hide behind safe-harbor intermediary defences to escape liability for illegal or unregulated products sold by third parties. Platforms must urgently tighten their vendor verification processes and listing audits to avoid heavy regulatory penalties.

ET BrandEquitySwiggy

Swiggy merges brand and performance marketing into a single strategy to drive growth

Swiggy recently identified a stagnation in its top-of-mind brand recall, which had remained flat for six to seven quarters. Traditionally, the food delivery platform treated brand and performance marketing as separate functions with distinct budgets, measurement metrics, and media buying strategies. This division led to a cycle of alternating priorities, shifting between brand investments and performance ROI.

To address this, Swiggy unified its brand and performance teams under a single revenue and growth framework with a shared budget. Speaking at ETBrandEquity’s DigiPlus Fest 2026, Anuj Gupta, vice president of food revenue and growth at Swiggy, explained that the new approach focused on crafting campaigns that simultaneously served brand recall and immediate conversions. Successful executions included creative interactions targeting Gen Z on platforms like Snapchat.

This unified strategy yielded measurable results for the platform, including a 6-point boost in Gen Z brand recall and a 15% reduction in customer acquisition costs.

Why it mattersMerging brand and performance marketing eliminates silos and optimizes budgets for a singular growth outcome. Marketers should consider treating brand equity and conversions as complementary forces within a single marketing funnel.
