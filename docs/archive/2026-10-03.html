The Verge AIOpenAI

OpenAI Introduces Dots: Enterprise-Focused AI Agents That Navigate Software and Virtual Machines

OpenAI has unveiled Dots, a new agentic platform featuring customizable, anthropomorphic avatars that can execute digital tasks by operating software directly on virtual machines. Unlike consumer-oriented alternatives like Meta's Muse, Dots is positioned primarily as enterprise software capable of acting as an autonomous coworker.

The system runs in a dedicated interface where users chat in one pane while observing the agent click and navigate on a virtual machine in another. Right out of the box, Dots can operate applications such as Blender and GIMP, link to a user's local setup through the ChatGPT desktop application, and handle voice interactions. However, real-world execution still faces hurdles, such as bot-detection mechanisms that require sustained human mouse inputs.

OpenAI is restricting initial access to its top-tier subscribers, such as those on its $100-per-month Pro tier, marking a distinct pricing strategy compared to free, ad-supported consumer alternatives. While users currently receive a single Dot, OpenAI plans to enable multi-agent setups in the future.

Why it mattersAutonomous software-navigating agents can streamline repetitive creative production and operational workflows across digital marketing tools. As agent-driven purchasing and web tasks grow, marketers must also anticipate how verification checks and bot controls will reshape the digital customer journey.

LivemintSocial, creators & culture

New FEMA Rules Mandate EDF Filings for Freelancers and YouTubers Earning in Dollars

Under updated Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA) rules effective October 1, Indian freelancers and content creators earning revenue in foreign currencies like US dollars must now file an Export Declaration Form (EDF) with their banks.

The regulatory measure impacts YouTubers and gig economy professionals receiving international remittances. Those earning foreign income will need to coordinate with their respective authorized dealer banks to complete the mandatory reporting and stay compliant with outbound trade and foreign exchange requirements.

Why it mattersBrands and agencies working with international and domestic creator networks must account for tighter compliance and potential payout processing adjustments for dollar-denominated earnings. The regulation may also accelerate the professionalisation of individual creators into structured, tax-compliant business entities.
