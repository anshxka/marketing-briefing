ET BrandEquityGoogle

AI Expands Indian Festive Consumer Journey to 13 Touchpoints, Says Google

Speaking at DigiPlus Fest 2026, Ankhee Bhakoo, Director of Google Customer Solutions at Google, detailed how artificial intelligence is transforming digital discovery ahead of India's festive shopping season. The festive period is projected to generate INR 1.2 lakh crore in Gross Merchandise Value (GMV), even as consumer interactions grow increasingly fragmented across multiple platforms.

Bhakoo explained that while Google is witnessing decades worth of technological innovation occur in a single day, consumer behaviour is moving even faster. Rather than shortening the path to purchase, AI has expanded it from an average of 5.5 touchpoints to nearly 13. According to presented data, 87% of AI users make faster and more confident choices during searches, while visual content steers 80% of shoppers during initial product discovery.

These consumer interactions are concentrated across four dominant activities: searching, streaming, scrolling, and shopping. Bhakoo noted that Google and YouTube provide comprehensive presence across all four stages, engaging nine out of 10 Gen Z consumers and 85% of internet users browsing from Tier 2 locations and beyond.

Why it mattersWith consumers consulting more than a dozen touchpoints before buying, brands cannot rely on single-channel or bottom-funnel performance marketing alone. Marketing teams must invest heavily in high-impact visual formats across search and streaming to remain visible throughout extended discovery cycles.

Automating the Content Supply Chain Is Crucial for Omnichannel Ad Success, Say Industry Leaders

At DigiPlus Fest 2026, executives from Biztechnosys Infotech and Sitecore highlighted that the next critical step in digital marketing lies in automating content supply chains. As brands commit more budget to programmatic advertising and influencer campaigns, internal workflows often face severe operational bottlenecks when creating, managing, and distributing assets across fragmented digital channels.

During a session on omnichannel orchestration, Kalpesh Vaza, MD and CEO of Biztechnosys Infotech, alongside CTO Manish Ramanan and Sitecore’s Abhiraj Banerjee, emphasized that reaching target audiences at the right moment remains a core industry challenge. Vaza argued that many brands struggle with connection because manual and disjointed creative processes weaken storytelling, content consistency, and human touch.

The panel urged marketing teams to optimize their backend creative engine before scaling external media investments. Automating the content pipeline reduces operational waste and ensures brand messaging stays cohesive across touchpoints, ultimately maximizing return on ad spend across human consumers as well as emerging AI agents.

Why it mattersScaling media spend without fixing manual creative production leads to bottlenecks and inconsistent cross-channel messaging. Marketers must modernize their internal content supply chains to deliver tailored assets rapidly without exhausting creative and operational resources.

ET BrandEquityConsuma

AI Rapid Research Aims to Bridge the Speed and Depth Divide in Consumer Insights

At DigiPlus Fest 2026, Siddhartha Sharma, Head of Sales at Consuma, highlighted how AI-native rapid research is helping brands keep pace with compressed consumer purchase journeys. With digital interactions multiplying, traditional market research methodologies are struggling to deliver actionable consumer intelligence fast enough.

Sharma noted that purchasing journeys that once took weeks or months now conclude in minutes. Modern shoppers frequently discover a product on an Instagram feed, review it on digital feedback forums, and place an order via quick commerce platforms within a single brief session. For brands, the immediate challenge is transforming this rapid stream of multi-format digital signals into meaningful business insights.

Historically, marketers faced an unavoidable trade-off between the rapid metrics provided by social listening platforms and the qualitative depth offered by legacy research firms like Nielsen or Kantar. Sharma noted that conventional social listening often acts merely as a scorecard, whereas AI-driven rapid research can synthesize multi-format digital data to deliver contextual, decision-ready insights in real time.

Why it mattersAs quick commerce compresses the consumer journey from discovery to checkout into minutes, marketers can no longer rely on research reports that take weeks to compile. Brands must adopt AI-powered rapid research to decode changing consumer sentiments and optimize campaign creative on the fly.
