Agency fees in creator deals emerge as the next transparency headache for marketers

As creator marketing commands a larger share of marketing budgets, it is facing the same transparency issues that previously hit programmatic advertising and principal media deals. Agencies are increasingly rolling talent fees and their own margins into a single bundled line item that clients cannot easily pick apart. Consultants are warning advertisers about this opacity, pointing out that marketers often have little idea where their creator money actually goes.

Data from a recent poll by the Responsible Marketing Advisory reveals the scale of the issue: just 42% of media leaders and creator forum members said their creator agency fees were fully transparent, while 21% said partially and 37% were unsure. Research from TrinityP3 shows that bundled creator deals can leave as little as 35% of the budget for talent, compared to up to 60% when work is billed line by line. Meanwhile, Association of National Advertisers figures indicate that agencies take an average of 30% of influencer spend, with only 39% of agreements being transparent.

Experts note that marketers are trading convenience for hidden fees by handing over lump sums to agencies managing large rosters of influencers. Moving forward, brands will need to demand greater accountability and unbundled pricing to ensure their creator budgets are spent efficiently.

Why it mattersPerformance and brand marketers must audit their influencer and creator agency contracts to eliminate hidden markups and ensure maximum budget goes directly to talent. Demand strict transparency to protect your ROI as creator investments scale.
