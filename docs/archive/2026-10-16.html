Inc42Flipkart

Flipkart Group FY26 Revenue Nears ₹1.5 Lakh Cr as Operational Losses Narrow

Walmart-backed Flipkart Group reported an 18.2% jump in consolidated revenue from operations to ₹1.45 Lakh Cr for fiscal year 2025-26, compared to ₹1.23 Lakh Cr in FY25. The consolidated entity—which incorporates Myntra, Cleartrip, logistics, and platform services—saw its consolidated net loss narrow marginally by 2.1% to ₹8,468 Cr, down from ₹8,646 Cr in the prior fiscal year. The results follow the completion of the group's reverse flip from Singapore to India in March 2026 under Flipkart Internet Private Limited ahead of a potential domestic public listing.

On the operational side, Flipkart saw its gross margins improve by 23% year-on-year, while adjusted EBITDA improved by roughly ₹1,500 Cr. Supported by higher gross margins and reduced overheads, its adjusted operating margin improved by 230 basis points from -7.7% to -5.4%. Adjusted operating business losses before finance costs, tax, exceptional items, and other income shrank 17% (around ₹1,600 Cr). However, broader net losses saw only a marginal reduction, weighed down by higher finance costs, exceptional items, and the derecognition of deferred tax assets.

In contrast to group-level growth, Flipkart's B2C arm, Flipkart Internet Private Limited, registered a 19.3% drop in revenue from operations to ₹16,295 Cr from ₹20,202 Cr in FY25. The B2C division's total income declined 19.4% to ₹17,039 Cr, while its total expenses dipped 3.6% during the fiscal year.

Why it mattersThe group's improving operating margins signal a push for disciplined unit economics as it prepares for an Indian IPO, despite a contraction in standalone B2C marketplace revenue. Performance marketers and marketplace sellers should anticipate tighter platform monetization and fee structures as Flipkart prioritizes profitability and enterprise ecosystem services.
