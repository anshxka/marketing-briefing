India Becomes ChatGPT's Largest Market Globally as User Base Doubles Since January

India has officially emerged as ChatGPT's largest market globally by weekly active users, with the local user base doubling since January 2026. According to OpenAI India Managing Director Prabhjeet Singh, this rapid acceleration is driven by Indian users integrating the chatbot into their daily workflows and finding diverse new applications. In February 2026, OpenAI reported having over 100 million weekly ChatGPT users in the country.

To capture audiences beyond major urban centers, OpenAI is aggressively expanding its Indian-language capabilities and focusing heavily on Android-first product development. The company is scaling its operational presence with new offices planned in Mumbai and Bengaluru alongside its Delhi NCR hub, while partnering with TCS’ HyperVault for massive local data center capacity ranging from 100 megawatts to 1 gigawatt to meet data residency needs.

Enterprise adoption is also shifting from experimental pilots to full production deployments across major Indian companies, global capability centers, and startups like JioHotstar and Cars24. Furthermore, OpenAI is engaging closely with government agencies and financial institutions to ensure security against AI agent misuse, while also introducing advertising on ChatGPT in India to support free and lower-priced tiers.

Why it mattersMarketers must recognize India as a premier, high-volume proving ground for consumer and enterprise AI adoption. The integration of advertising on platforms like ChatGPT opens up brand-new frontier channels for engagement.

TechCrunch AIGoogle

Google Launches Experimental AI App Dreambeans in India as First International Market

Google has chosen India as the first international market outside the U.S. for its experimental AI app, Dreambeans. Available on Android and iOS, the app launches with support for 13 local languages including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, and Bengali, alongside English. Dreambeans automatically generates personalized, illustrated visual stories based on a user's digital activity across connected Google services like Calendar, Gmail, Photos, Search, and YouTube, without requiring manual prompts.

Google selected India due to overwhelming local interest and a high overlap of active users across its ecosystem services. Recently, Google updated the app to include Veo video-generation capabilities to animate images within select stories. Following its U.S. launch in June, Dreambeans experienced fluctuating downloads, accumulating roughly 55,000 total downloads, and faces distinct adoption patterns compared to general-purpose conversational AI apps.

Looking ahead, Google plans to scale the app's footprint and feature set globally while gauging user engagement across India's linguistically diverse consumer base.

Why it mattersMarketers should note Google's hyper-localized product deployment strategy, which uses native language support and high-penetration ecosystem overlap to test engagement features in India first. This signals a broader industry shift toward proactive, zero-prompt AI content experiences driven by personal consumer data.
